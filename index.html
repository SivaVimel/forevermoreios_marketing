Made to be yours

Everything you need to count down to the day you say “I do”.

Your title, your way

Style each word with Normal, Italic or an elegant Script typeface, in any colour. Soft gold by default.

Widgets

Small, medium and large on your Home Screen, plus circle, rectangle and inline styles on your Lock Screen.

Six soft backgrounds

Pearl, Blush, Sage, Mist, Dusk and Midnight, or your own photo. Used across the app, widgets and wallpaper.

Gentle reminders

Milestones at 100, 50, 30, 14, 7, 3 and 1 day to go, an optional daily reminder, and a note when the day arrives.

Celebration wallpaper

Save a wallpaper made from your title and date, and set it on the day with a simple Shortcuts automation.

And after the day

Once you're married, the countdown becomes a gentle count of the days since you said “I do”.

Private by design.

No account, no sign-in, no ads and no tracking. Everything stays on your iPhone.
